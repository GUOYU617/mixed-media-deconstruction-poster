# Position-Lock Quality Gate

Inspect the actual source and output before reporting success.

## Source analysis

- Dimensions, ratio, horizon or pose axis, main silhouettes, vanishing directions, overlaps, and intersections come from the inspected source.
- Every zone has an original location and A/B/C priority.
- Every A or B region is visible in the source; no hidden structure is invented.
- C regions exclude identity-bearing or geometry-bearing content unless the user requested omission.
- At least one retained-photo A region contains explicit story evidence rather than only generic texture.
- The palette record identifies source-derived dominant, support, accent, and neutral colors.

## Critical spatial checks

Treat any failure here as central:

- The source crop logic, camera viewpoint, horizon, perspective, and overall composition remain recognizable.
- Every important object or body part remains at the same source-relative position.
- No part is independently enlarged, reduced, rotated, shifted, detached, duplicated, or displayed as a separate study.
- Relative scale, overlap order, object count, pose, and adjacency remain unchanged.
- Rooflines, roads, rails, wires, limbs, tools, edges, and other declared continuation lines meet across media boundaries without jumps.
- The retained photographic region and illustrated regions depict one continuous scene, not different copies of the scene.
- The result is not divided into upper/lower, left/right, before/after, equal-band, or photo-next-to-drawing sections.

## Media and whitespace checks

- Requested media are visibly distinct through physical mark behavior, not separate layout boxes.
- Each medium appears over its assigned original region.
- Torn edges, washes, and fades reveal transitions without displacing content.
- Hard torn edges follow or respond to named source contours such as trees, roofs, shorelines, ridges, roads, garments, or silhouettes; they do not arbitrarily bisect important forms.
- The result contains 2–4 clearly visible in-place torn-paper seams; fewer than two or more than four fails the gate.
- Every seam shows irregular white paper fibers, a slight lifted lip, perceptible paper thickness, and a very shallow natural shadow. Watercolor feathering, pigment bloom, dry fade, or a blurred mask alone does not count.
- Different declared media meet on the two sides of every seam, and the registered source geometry continues through it.
- At 25% viewing scale, at least two seams still read immediately as physical torn paper.
- Any paper-layer overlap remains in place. No fragment is translated, rotated, independently scaled, detached, floating, or sticker-like.
- Whitespace occurs at the perimeter or inside C regions and does not erase required structure.
- Visible whitespace covers at least 20% of the total canvas. Count bare neutral paper and only extremely faint, incomplete graphite or watercolor residue; exclude dense illustration, photographic texture, decorative distress, and merely pale-colored filled regions.
- Whitespace is visibly generous, calm, and cleaner than the source rather than being filled with decorative debris.
- Low-priority detail may become faint or incomplete; important forms remain readable at thumbnail scale.
- One paper surface, restrained palette, and reproduction treatment unify the image.
- Natural source colors remain recognizable and neutrals remain neutral. Reject a global yellow, sepia, brown, orange, heavily faded, distressed, or antique cast unless explicitly requested.
- No unwanted text, copied reference caption, platform watermark, logo, signature, fictional internal, or random object appears.
- The result avoids specimen boards, exploded views, orbiting fragments, equal panels, dense scrapbook styling, glossy mockups, cinematic depth, and 3D rendering.

## Delivery decision

If a central check fails, revise only the failed spatial constraint and regenerate once. Prioritize:

1. position, perspective, scale, and count;
2. identity, pose, and structural continuity;
3. correct registered medium assignment;
4. controlled whitespace;
5. texture and optional typography.

Treat a whitespace share below 20%, fewer than 2 or more than 4 visible torn-paper seams, missing physical tear cues, same-medium seam sides, stacked photo-plus-illustration layout, arbitrary structural tear, moved or floating fragments, or global vintage color cast as a central failure requiring the one targeted regeneration.

If the second result still fails, return the better artifact with a precise limitation. Never call a repositioned or duplicated result position-locked.

## Required delivery record

Report output path, source role, spatial preservation status, registered zone-to-medium map, transition pattern, retained-photo share, estimated whitespace share, whitespace plan, regeneration status, final prompt, and remaining limitation.
